About

A disciplined approach to financial analysis

I am an undergraduate from Edinburgh Napier University with a focus on financial modelling, equity research, and fundamental valuation. I believe that rigorous analysis and clear communication are the cornerstones of sound investment decision-making.

Having completed the CFA Investment Foundations certification recently, during the summer I look forward to completing the CFI Financial Modeling & Valuation Analyst certification. I am building a body of applied work that demonstrates my ability to construct institutional-grade models and produce actionable equity research.

This portfolio is a living record of the analytical work I produce, the frameworks I develop, and the investment thinking I refine over time.
